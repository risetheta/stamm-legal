Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how the Stamm app ("the app") handles your data. The app is made by Robin Bosse ("I"). Questions about privacy: risetheta@gmail.com.

In short

The app does not send or share any personal data, so nothing from the app reaches me. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What the app stores on your device

To work, the app saves two things in its own storage on your device:

  • your learning progress: which words you have studied, when each one is due again, how your answers went, and daily counts for the statistics and streak;
  • your settings: learning direction, study mode, daily limits, theme and sound.

This data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. The app never sends it anywhere, and I cannot see it.

If you have turned on Android backup, Android includes this data in your phone's backup to your own Google account, as it does for most apps. On Android 9 and later the backup is end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock, and it lets your progress come back when you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. I never receive this backup.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Settings → Reset progress deletes your progress; clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes everything on the device. If Android backup is on, the latest backup copy stays in your Google account until Google replaces or removes it, or you delete your device backup there.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google handles that under its own privacy policy. Google shows me general statistics about the app (such as installs and ratings), the reviews you choose to post, and crash reports from people who allow their device to share diagnostics with Google. I use these only to keep the app working and improve it, which is my legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

If you contact me

Emailing me is voluntary. If you do, I use your address and message only to reply to you, and delete them when the conversation is finished and I no longer need them. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering questions about the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The messages are stored by my email provider, Google (Gmail), which handles them for me.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what personal data I hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict how I use it, and receive a copy of it. Apart from emails you send me and what Google Play shows me, I hold none. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

Right to object: where I rely on my legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object at any time, for reasons arising from your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). Email me and I will stop.

Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, I will update this page and the date at the top.
